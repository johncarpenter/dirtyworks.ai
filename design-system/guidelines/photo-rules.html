SHOW
  • People absorbed in real work — never looking at camera.
  • Experienced workers and mixed-age teams.
  • Hands marking, comparing, teaching, testing, resolving.
  • Paper and digital tools in the same frame.
  • Real material: worn wood, binders, wool, steel, cables.
  • Hard side light or direct flash, subtle grain, real skin texture.
  • Alberta through winter light and modest offices — not landmarks.
  • Negative space that can take a solid ink or bone capsule.
AVOID
  • Smiling or pointing at camera; handshakes, high-fives, lineups.
  • Glowing brains, robots, holograms, neon, purple gradients.
  • Glass technology campuses and identical young startup teams.
  • Fake PPE, hard hats indoors, oil-derrick shorthand for Calgary.
  • Readable confidential documents, credentials, logos, vendor UI.
  • Soft protection gradients over photography.
  • Any image presented as customer proof without a release.
